# Internal Note: General AI Market Trends (illustrative, adjacent)

> Sample/synthetic data for the course. This note is intentionally *adjacent* —
> it discusses AI broadly, not coding agents specifically, and acts as a
> semantic distractor for retrieval benchmarks.

## Broad themes

- Foundation models keep improving on reasoning and long context, lifting every
  downstream application including, but not limited to, developer tools.
- Costs per token continue to fall, shifting differentiation away from raw model
  access and toward product, data, and workflow integration.
- Enterprises are standing up internal AI platforms, model gateways, and usage
  governance independent of any single vendor.

## Why this is a distractor

Much of this vocabulary — "enterprise", "model", "governance", "reliability" —
overlaps the coding-agent notes, so naive retrieval can surface this general
note when the user actually wants coding-agent specifics. Good retrieval should
prefer the on-topic sources.
