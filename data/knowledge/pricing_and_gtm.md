# Internal Note: Pricing & Go-To-Market (illustrative)

> Sample/synthetic data for the course.

## Pricing shapes seen in the market

- **Per-seat**: predictable for buyers, but caps upside and invites "how many
  seats actually use it?" scrutiny.
- **Usage-based**: aligns cost with value on heavy-automation workloads, but
  makes budgets unpredictable — a friction point for enterprise procurement.
- **Hybrid platform fee + usage**: an emerging compromise.

## Go-to-market notes

- Bottom-up developer adoption lowers CAC but rarely clears enterprise security
  review on its own; a top-down motion is still needed to land regulated buyers.
- A design-partner program that co-develops governance features doubles as
  product validation and as a reference-customer engine.
- Land-and-expand works only if the first team's results are measurable — which
  loops back to reliability and evaluation as the real product.
